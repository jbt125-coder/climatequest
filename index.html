Camp break!

Help Dr. Frost around camp. Penguins slide in and Weddell seals pop up through breathing holes in the ice. Toss a fish to a hungry animal to earn a heart. Fish bounce and slide on the ice, so a fish that slides right up to an animal counts too. Don't let one slide into an empty breathing hole!

Penguins on top of the glacier cliff are worth 2 hearts. The cliff is too high to climb, so jump and toss in the air for a jump shot.

Controls: the arrow pad runs left and right. A (or up) jumps; press it again in the air to double jump. Hold B to fill the power meter, then let go to toss. Green is a short, soft toss and red is a long, hard one. The fish flies the way Dr. Frost is facing.

Watch out for south polar skuas! Their poop costs a heart. If your fish hits a skua, it gets mad and swoops at you. Jump on a skua to bop it for a heart. A fish that bounces off a skua and still gets eaten is worth 2 hearts.

You have 1 minute. After a round, the camp is closed for 10 minutes.

Time to get to work!

Fun fact: this game is just for fun. In real Antarctica, the Antarctic Treaty makes it against the rules to feed or disturb wildlife, so scientists keep their distance. Penguins and seals live along the coast, far from inland drill camps. But south polar skuas really do dive at people who get too close to their nests!
